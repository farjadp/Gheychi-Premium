Why

Built because the alternatives were bad.

Downloader sites are a genre: five ad slots, three fake buttons, a redirect, and a file that may or may not be the one you asked for. The tool ought to be smaller than the page selling it.

Putting it in Telegram removes the website from the transaction entirely. You are already in the chat; the file arrives in the chat. This site exists to explain that, not to host it.
