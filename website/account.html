Common questions
A file was too big

Telegram caps what a bot can send. Oversized files are relayed another way when possible, and when they cannot be, you get the original link instead.

My plan did not activate

Card payments activate automatically within a minute. If it has been longer than that, message support with the date on the invoice.

A private Telegram channel will not download

Our Telegram account has to be a member of that channel or group first. Invite links of the form t.me/+hash are not supported.

I want to use a second Telegram account

Link it under the Telegram tab. How many you can link depends on your plan, and they all share one quota pool rather than each getting their own.
