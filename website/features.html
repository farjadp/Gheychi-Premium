CapabilitiesGheychi Premium

Everything it handles,
and where it stops.

The honest list. Anything absent from this page is something the bot does not do yet, and the limits below are the real ones.

Group ATelegram

Restricted content

The reason this bot exists. A channel owner can switch off forwarding and saving; the ordinary Bot API then cannot read those messages at all. Gheychi runs a real user session over MTProto instead, so the post is just a message.

Private channels and groups

Any chat the bot's account can see, including ones with content protection enabled.

Forum topics

Links carrying a topic id resolve correctly, not just the root channel.

Whole albums

A media group arrives complete, in order, rather than as the single message you linked.

Every media type

Video, photo, audio, voice notes, video notes, animations and plain documents.

Not supported

Invite links of the form t.me/+hash cannot be resolved. Paste a link to an individual post instead. The bot's account must already be a member of the channel — it cannot join one for you.

Group BOpen web

The rest of the internet

1000+ sites

Anything yt-dlp supports. The named platforms below are the ones tuned and tested; the long tail generally works too.

Quality selection

Available resolutions are read from the source and listed as buttons. No guessing, no fixed ladder.

Audio extraction

Any video becomes an MP3. SoundCloud and RadioJavan are handled as audio directly.

Multi-layer fetching

A direct API path is tried first where it is faster, with yt-dlp as the fallback that always works.
