Questions

Before you commit

Does a paid plan give me bigger files?

No. 50 MB is Telegram's ceiling for bot uploads and applies to every tier. Plans change quantity and platform access, never file size.

What happens when a quota runs out?

The bot declines that platform and tells you when the period resets. Other platforms keep working on their own counters.

Can I change plans?

Yes, through support. Because plans are applied manually, a change takes effect as soon as it is processed rather than at a billing boundary.

Do you store what I download?

The file is deleted from the server within the hour. Your history keeps the link and the timestamp so you can see your own usage.
